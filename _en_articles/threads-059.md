In the previous two posts, we talked about how even FBI agents use the "friendship formula" to win the goodwill, even the cooperation, of adversaries under conditions of extreme distrust.

In daily life, as long as you use this formula on the right people, in the right way,
it's like flipping the "like switch" in someone's heart: no forcing, no people-pleasing,
you're simply liked and approached naturally.

The friendship formula:
proximity × frequency × duration × intensity

But if you apply it to Zi Wei Dou Shu, you'll find that each major star calls for a completely different approach.
I'll teach you each star's "way of liking" and its "own icebreaker lines," so you can read everyone's code for getting along faster.
Continuing with the other major stars:

║ The 14 major stars × The Like Switch
8 | The Moon (Tai Yin): make me feel safe, and only then will I slowly come closer
The Moon is sensitive and delicate, and needs "safety" and "to be understood."
Don't move too fast, don't press with questions, don't attack its feelings.
Key to friendship: gentleness, understanding, patience, emotional attunement.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: show up in gentle settings (cafés, quiet activities).
• Frequency: short, frequent interactions work very well.
• Duration: quiet companionship builds familiarity faster than chatting.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "You looked a little unwell just now; are you OK?"
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "You're so caring and attentive; I think that's wonderful." "I've noticed everything you put in."

9 | The Strategist (Tian Ji): keep up with my train of thought, and only then will I open up
The Strategist likes "being understood" and "a meeting of minds," and needs a flowing exchange.
If the conversation hits the right "points," it will get close to you.
Key to friendship: asking questions, listening, understanding, conversation.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: it's easiest to get familiar in settings where you can chat.
• Frequency: medium frequency = safe.
• Duration: if the conversation flows, it will treat you as a friend.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "I'm curious why you thought that just now?"
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "Your view makes a lot of sense, but I think XX; what do you think?" "Your ideas are really insightful."

10 | The Guardian (Tian Liang): respect me and trust me, and I'll be willing to look after you
The Guardian is like a big brother or big sister, and likes people who are mature and can carry responsibility.
The steadier and more mature you are, the more it appreciates you.
Key to friendship: trust, respect, maturity.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: it's easiest to get close in situations where "help is needed."
• Frequency: steady, not abrupt, not frivolous.
• Duration: handling things together is the fastest way to get familiar.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "You handled that so maturely just now; I really admire it."
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "You're very trustworthy." "Having you around puts people at ease."

11 | The Star of Obscurity (Ju Men): accept my insecurity, and I'll be interested in you
The Star of Obscurity has a lot of mental theater going on, but that doesn't mean it's negative. Its fear comes from "opacity"; it hopes that when it gives sincerely, it will get the response it expects. It likes "sincerity" and "people who respond."

Key to friendship: being genuine, interacting, being able to discuss.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: settings with chatting and interaction work best.
• Frequency: frequent conversation = trust.
• Duration: once you can't stop talking, you're friends.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "That wisecrack you just made was hilarious; I loved it."
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "Your analysis just now was spot on." "I like that you tell the truth."

12 | The Blessing Star (Tian Tong): make me feel relaxed, and I'll lean on you
The Blessing Star doesn't like pressure; it likes being relaxed and hates being tired; it "works" smart and wants to rely on others.
Key to friendship: ease, cuteness, no pressure.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: relaxed places with a good atmosphere are the easiest to get familiar in.
• Frequency: small interactions are important.
• Duration: being able to go out eating, drinking and having fun together.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "Which dessert did you just have? It looked so good."
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "What you said just now was so cute, it made me laugh." "I feel really comfortable around you."

13 | The Seeker (Tan Lang): make me feel you're fun, and I'll come find you to play
The Seeker is a peach blossom star and a star that dispels hardship, full of curiosity, wanting to experience everything; it likes "charm," "fun" and "social flow."
If you can light up its interests, it will make a point of getting close to you.

Key to friendship: fun, lots of topics, charm.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: music, parties and social settings are where you're most likely to meet it.
• Frequency: frequent, relaxed interactions work best.
• Duration: if the conversation takes off = you'll get close fast.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "I just saw you holding a diving form. Diving is so much fun."
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "I heard you want to try diving. We're heading out to sea on Saturday; interested?"

14 | The Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen): if you're not boring, then I'll say a few more words to you
The Star of Restraint cares about image, has style and strong charm, and likes clever, sparky interactions.
You need to be able to catch its jokes, and not make it "lose face."
Key to friendship: charm, style, tension, efficiency.
Most effective strategy:
• Proximity: places with eye contact, exchange and presence (parties, cafés, exhibitions).
• Frequency: medium-frequency interaction is just right: not clingy, not cold, with rhythm.
• Duration: doing things together, discussing together, interactions with tension = the fastest way to get close.
• Intensity:
Breaking the ice with a stranger: "That response of yours was just right; very impressive."
Breaking the ice with a weak tie: "I admire your style of getting things done; very decisive." "The way you handled that was so slick; I'd love to learn from you."

Everyone likes to be understood, to be seen, and to be approached "in the right way."
The FBI agent's friendship formula × the major stars of Zi Wei Dou Shu: as long as you use the right way of interacting,
you don't have to change yourself, and people will naturally like you.
