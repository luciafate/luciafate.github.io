Continuing from the last post, we said: "outcome = person × environment × timing."
Everything that happens is the result of how you interact with the outside world: in "what kind of environment," in "what way," at "what point in time."

To change the outcome, you have to change these three parameters. Don't want to rely on willpower? Then the way that works immediately is to start with "changing your environment."

In today's post, I'll take you from the perspective of feng shui + psychology to build an environment and space that gets you moving without relying on willpower.

※ It's not that you lack self-discipline; you've been tamed by your space: the power of place
Have you noticed that the same you seems like a different person in different spaces?
Back home, you collapse like a dead fish;
at the office, you instantly switch into work mode;
lying in bed, you start scrolling on your phone.
Besides willpower, there's also "the power of place" at work here.

Feng shui is really the environment we live in,
and the environment directly affects our mood and behavior.
This has long been confirmed in psychology's stimulus–response model.

When we repeatedly carry out certain behaviors in a particular space,
that time and space takes on a strong power of "place."

For example, many highly effective people create a dedicated workspace for themselves.
As soon as they enter this space,
their body and mind immediately go into work mode;
as soon as they leave it, body and mind automatically switch to a relaxed state.
So the influence of environment on behavior is extremely significant.
