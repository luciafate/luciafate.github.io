║ The palace system
A Zi Wei Dou Shu chart has twelve palaces in total. Each palace represents a life situation and set of environmental conditions,
covering what we care about over a lifetime:
family, love, career, finances, health, the inner life, and interactions with others.

The twelve palaces are arranged clockwise in a fixed order:
Palace of Self → Palace of Parents and Elders → Palace of Inner Life and Fortune → Palace of Home and Property → Palace of Daily Focus → Palace of Friends and Associates → Palace of the Outer World → Palace of Body and Health → Palace of Money Management → Palace of Children and Creation → Palace of Love and Partnership → Palace of Siblings → back to the Palace of Self.

Palace of Self: personality, values, abilities, appearance and inherited traits; the core of the whole chart, your main personality.
Palace of the Outer World: your outward image, how you behave, your interactions with people out in the world, and the impression others have of you.
Palace of Parents and Elders: heredity, looks, your relationship with your father, family background, upbringing.
Palace of Body and Health: heredity, looks, your attitude to using your body, illness, misfortune.
Palace of Inner Life and Fortune: mental state, the subconscious, the soul, values, luck, how you earn and spend money.
Palace of Money Management: money-management ability, patterns of income and spending, attitude to money, and financial fortune.
Palace of Home and Property: your source of security, home environment, feng shui, relationships with family members, and assets, savings and wealth reserves.
Palace of Children and Creation: your ideas about raising children, parent-child interaction, attitude to sex life, your creations, your possessions and pets.
