The logic of the Palace of Self's san fang si zheng, seen through the greatest manager in Dream of the Red Chamber: Wang Xifeng

In the huge "family business" of the Jia household, there were only some 30 masters, but as many as 300 to 400 staff below them, a span of management comparable to a large conglomerate. Personnel assignments, allocation of resources, risk control: every day there were hundreds of matters large and small to handle. That such a complex organization could run stably was all down to Wang Xifeng, this "senior manager who best understood power and interests."

From Wang Xifeng's daily operations we can see her personality traits, and work backward to the structure of her Palace of Self's san fang si zheng.
1\. The parachuted-in general manager: connections and skill were her foot in the door
Wang Xifeng originally belonged to Jia Lian's branch of the family, but through her mother's side (Lady Wang was her own aunt) she was "parachuted in" as manager of the Rongguo Mansion, holding the core resources and the center of power.
But holding on to that position took more than connections; it took real ability. Wang Xifeng was bold and fiery, shrewd and capable, conventionally beautiful, and extremely skilled with people. With those above her, she knew how to please the elders and win their trust; with those below, she combined favor and severity, calming people and putting things in order. Who was talking behind whose back, who was making moves: she knew it all, and turning the tables or bringing people over to her side was her specialty.
A few passages of the original dialogue show her managerial skill:
"Though our family isn't what it was in the great days of our founding ancestors, from top to bottom we still have three or four hundred people. Even if there isn't much going on, there are still ten or twenty matters a day, all like a tangle of hemp, with no thread to take hold of as the main line."
The dialogue between You Erjie and Shanjie, the maid Wang Xifeng assigned to her:

You Erjie said: "I've run out of hair oil. Go and tell the senior mistress and fetch some."
Shanjie said: "Second mistress, how can you be so ungrateful and blind to the situation? Our mistress waits on the old lady every day, and has to wait on the lady here and the lady there too. All these sisters-in-law and sisters, hundreds of men and women high and low, get up every day waiting for her word. On a single day, at the very least, there are ten or twenty big matters, and thirty or fifty small ones. Outside, starting from the Imperial Consort, there are all the social courtesies with the households of princes, dukes, marquises and earls; at home, there's the arranging of all these relatives and friends. Silver by the thousand and cash by the ten thousand, every day it's all arranged by her one pair of hands, one mind and one mouth. How could she be bothered over a little thing like this? I advise you to make do."

2\. Off-the-charts planning ability: turning a funeral into a full-scale show of management
Her first big task after being parachuted in was presiding over Qin Keqing's funeral. It was a project mobilizing a hundred people. She held meetings, divided people into groups, set responsibilities and scheduled progress, even ruling:
"If a single cup is broken, the culprit can be caught right away and must pay for it at once, no excuses."
This project-management ability makes her an ancient master PM. Barely in her early twenties, she built order through systems, punishments and rewards, a classic authoritarian leader: quick results, clear discipline, very high obedience from the team. And this made her name in one stroke.

3\. A financial expert: lending household funds at high interest, skilled at compounding it
Wang Xifeng didn't just know how to manage people; she was very good at managing money too. Her hidden method was diverting household funds to lend out at interest:

The money that should have gone each month to the servants and to household expenses, she first lent out at high interest, keeping the interest for herself and making up the accounts at the end of the month. This kind of operation is more seasoned than many modern bosses.
She even forged documents and took bribes for meddling in lawsuits, an ancient expert in risk arbitrage.

4\. Purging the mistress
When her husband Jia Lian secretly took You Erjie as a wife, Wang Xifeng didn't openly tear into him; instead she brought the other woman down through a series of psychological and procedural tactics: first taking her in under the name of the Rong household, then scheming so that the mistress miscarried and was finally driven to suicide, and the estate even went "legally" into her own pocket.
As a principal wife who hadn't borne a son (she had only one daughter, Qiaojie), she still held on firmly to her high position and control of the household. What she relied on was capability, precision and ruthlessness: who should be promoted, who punished, who was a confidant, who a mole, she knew it all clearly. Whenever anyone challenged her order of control, she never went easy.

🔸 The Palace of Self and its san fang si zheng small team
Wang Xifeng's Self, Outer World, Money and Daily Focus palaces are what support her whole underlying personality:
[Palace of Self] inborn nature and abilities
[Palace of the Outer World] inner thoughts, how she interacts with the outside world, the impression she gives
[Palace of Money Management] values about money and command of resources
[Palace of Daily Focus] workplace structure, responsibilities and ambition

Her "strength" isn't personality, but a whole system of judging people, allocating resources and operating systems. This is what san fang si zheng means: the small team in the chart is the real force behind success.
