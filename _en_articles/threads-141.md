Many Zi Wei Dou Shu beginners get stuck on the concept of "san fang si zheng" and don't understand why reading one palace means referring to so many places.
Actually it's very intuitive: in real life, a person's behavior is never determined by "personality" alone; it's shaped jointly by many factors, such as their worldview, outlook on life and values, their attitude to money, and what they care about.
In the chart, these interwoven inner and outer sides are exactly the "small team" formed by four palaces: the Palace of Self, the Outer World, Money Management and Daily Focus:
•	Palace of Self: inborn personality and abilities
•	Palace of the Outer World: your inner thoughts, how you present yourself, how others see you
•	Palace of Money Management: how you use resources, and how your attitude to money and spiritual values show
•	Palace of Daily Focus: the focus and pursuits of your daily life

Every palace has its own san fang si zheng partners:
•	The fifth palace clockwise is this palace's Palace of Daily Focus, representing this palace's focus and everyday pursuits
•	The fifth palace counterclockwise is this palace's Palace of Money Management, which can give this palace resources and a source of life (its resources and nourishment come from here)
•	The opposite palace is its Palace of the Outer World: this palace's inner thoughts and outward expression

So when reading a palace, you can't take one palace and one star on their own; you have to look at how the whole group interacts. Once you get the hang of thinking in san fang si zheng, the chart goes from flat to three-dimensional, from single points to a network.

If you want to quickly see a person's underlying personality and life focus: first grab the star combinations of the Self, Outer World, Money and Daily Focus palaces.

Just like Wang Xifeng in Dream of the Red Chamber: just by looking at her Self, Money and Daily Focus palaces, you can clearly sketch out her character: clever, domineering, understanding people's hearts, skilled at pulling the strings.
