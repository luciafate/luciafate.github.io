In middle school physics we learned this:
when you apply a force F to an object and it moves a distance d in some direction, you have done "work."
But if the angle between the force and the direction of motion is 90 degrees, cos(90°) = 0,
and no matter how hard you push, the work done is still zero.

That is effort with nothing to show for it.

And in real life?
You might work overtime night after night, and your name still isn't on the promotion list.
You might pour yourself into a relationship, only to hear, "We're just not right for each other."
It isn't that you didn't try.
It's that your effort wasn't aimed in the direction it needed to go.

✅ In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the stars that leave you feeling you've been busy for nothing are Kong Jie: the pair Di Kong and Di Jie.
Kong Jie keep the stars around them from exerting their strength, yet they are also genuinely able to restrain the Sha stars.
Kong Jie make the whole palace an ineffective environment. Whatever stars sit in that palace, however strong, cannot take effect in that area of life.
Even if the stars in the palace are excellent, and the timing cycles bring Hua Lu, Hua Quan and Hua Ke, as long as Kong Jie are there, all that potential lands as if on barren ground: highly capable, with nowhere to put it to use.

Kong Jie are placed by your hour of birth, and they form four kinds of arrangements:
🔸 Same palace: the strongest disruption. Born in the Wu hour or the Zi hour, Kong Jie sit together in the Si or Hai position.
🔸 Facing each other: two palaces lose effect. Born in the Mao or You hour, they sit in the Yin and Shen positions.
🔸 Trine: they disrupt the palace at the center. For example, Di Kong and Di Jie in the Chou and You positions converge on the Si position (the palace affected).

🔸 Flanking: an attack from both sides. For example, Kong Jie in the Zi and Xu positions flank the Hai position (the palace affected).

✅ Kong Jie make stars ineffective through the palace environment, but the stars' influence remains!
For example, suppose Di Kong and Di Jie sit with the Strategist (Tian Ji) in the Hai palace, and that palace happens to be your Palace of Inner Life and Fortune.
The Strategist, a star that values order, reason and seeking better ways, loses its effect in the area of "how you think, your inner spirit, your outlook on life." But! The Strategist still affects its trine palaces, its opposite palace, and indeed the other eleven palaces of the chart.
The star's power is still there; it simply cannot act directly within the palace it belongs to.

✅ Kong Jie are felt most when they share a palace or face each other, often as the pain of "nothing I do makes a difference."
For example:
🔸 Kong Jie with the Strategist in your Palace of Money Management: everyone says you're rational, calm and good at planning, yet you always buy stocks at the top. You're not actually impulsive; the Strategist simply "can't take effect when it comes to managing money."
🔸 Kong Jie with Zi Wei–Qi Sha (the Emperor with the Resolute Star) in your Palace of Daily Focus: you think on a large scale and get things done, and family and friends see you as a natural leader, yet your colleagues and managers at the office just won't recognize it!

✅ In the timing cycles, it may all come to nothing in the end
When Kong Jie fall on the Partnership–Daily Focus axis (the Palace of Love and Partnership and the Palace of Daily Focus) in a ten-year cycle, it suggests that over those ten years your relationship and career may end up empty. The process may be sweet, you may truly have had it, the effort may be real, but the ending leaves you asking, "How did it all just disappear?"

✅ If you've noticed that lately your efforts keep coming to nothing, open your chart and see whether Kong Jie are sitting in that palace.
Don't lose heart. Heaven is only reminding you: "The direction is wrong; push as hard as you like and it won't move."
The moment you become aware of this is the beginning of changing your fortune.

Kong Jie keep good qualities from taking effect, but they also lessen the harm of the Sha stars and Hua Ji.
They make you miss some things, and they also keep you from walking into disasters you shouldn't walk into.

What you lost may simply not have been yours to have.
