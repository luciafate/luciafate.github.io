The most heart-wrenching scene in Love's Ambition: Xu Yan lowers herself, apologizes and declares her love, only to be coldly rejected by Shen Haoming: "You might as well be after fame or money! At least then you'd seem more real to me!"
She realizes she isn't loved, that she's just one link in a transactional relationship. She cries as she eats, pretending to be calm until she finally breaks down. That self-mocking, clear-eyed pain reminded countless people of their own moments of heartbreak.

So how do you heal yourself when your heart is broken? Psychology offers three steps:
① Acknowledge the pain: allow yourself to feel the loss and to cry; you don't have to act tough.
② Steady your body and mind: regular sleep, deep breathing, less stimulation, to pull yourself back.
③ Mobilize resources & rebuild connection: call up memories and strengths of "I can do this," and put social support or professional help into your schedule.

║ Zi Wei Dou Shu
The 14 major stars have different tendencies in recovery. Which kind of self-healing is yours?

The Emperor (Zi Wei) | Restoring order and dignity
Pain point: loss of face, wounded self-esteem
First aid: write down "3 small things I can control today," do only those 3, and regain your self-affirmation
Long term: first draw boundaries, then put your life in order, then deliver results; finish small things every week, and win back your dignity with visible achievements

The Strategist (Tian Ji) | Sorting out the chaos by thinking
Pain point: replaying it in your head, over-analyzing, thinking late into the night, endlessly questioning
First aid: rationally make a list of "emotions, evidence, and verifiable next actions," and stop chasing the unknown
Long term: outdoor activities or exercise twice a week, to give your brain "downtime"; at night, stop questioning and get a good sleep
