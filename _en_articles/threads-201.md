There are many kinds of scams, and some charts really are especially prone to falling into "investment scam" traps. Although all 14 major stars have "the potential to be fooled," watch out for these combinations!
1\. The overconfident type: the Strategist (Tian Ji), the Guardian (Tian Liang), the Chancellor (Tian Fu), the Sun (Tai Yang), the Emperor (Zi Wei), the Star of Depletion (Po Jun)
→ They think they understand it and control the whole picture, but their gift for filling in the logic themselves is too strong, so they're actually easily taken in
2\. Wanting easy money, not wanting to work too hard: pleasure-seeking stars like the Blessing Star (Tian Tong), the Guardian and the Moon (Tai Yin) (especially in the Palace of Money Management)
→ No resistance to "passive income," "an easy million," "just scroll on your phone"
3\. The type who lets others decide (relying on connections): the Keeper of the Seal (Tian Xiang) family (especially Wu Qu–Tian Xiang, the Treasurer with the Keeper of the Seal), or the Seeker (Tan Lang), the Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen), and stars of the Water element
→ "My friend said so" or "the group chat recommended it" easily becomes the basis for decisions
4\. The charge-to-the-end type (who even yells at the police): the Resolute Star (Qi Sha) family, especially Wu Qu–Qi Sha (the Treasurer with the Resolute Star)
→ Once they've made up their mind, they push through to the end and won't listen to anyone's advice, feeling "you're blocking my road to riches"
5\. Wanting more (the greedy type): the Seeker with Hua Ji + Sha stars (want it, want it, still want it)
→ Excited by an enticing opportunity, and with Sha stars and Hua Ji coming from the environment, the chance of being fooled multiplies!

When these stars appear in: the Self–Outer World axis (personality and abilities, judgment), the Palace of Money Management and the Palace of Inner Life and Fortune (money-management ability and values, how money comes in, and wealth luck), the Partnership–Daily Focus axis (life focus),
together with: Sha stars and Hua Ji, legal trouble, a poor Siblings–Friends axis, useless teammates in your circle of connections,
and then a sign of financial loss on top… be very careful!

Zi Wei Dou Shu can warn you of the "risks" in your fortune. Losing money to a scam doesn't mean your fate is bad; it means it's time to wake up!
