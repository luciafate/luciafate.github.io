"Family love and friendship can't replace romantic love. Love is a basic human need, not a bonus; it's life's safe harbor."
Being single isn't frightening, but if you long for love, "being happily single" is only a temporary state, not the final goal.
In his book The Single Trap, British relationship counselor Andrew Marshall sums up his 30 years of experience helping single people:

║ Why is it so hard to find a partner?
It's not that you're not good enough; it's that you're heading in the wrong direction.
In his view, the key reason modern people find it hard to get out of singlehood lies in two traps:

① Too many choices → unable to decide
In the age of dating apps, you can swipe through thousands of potential partners. With more choices, you become afraid of missing someone better, and fall into "the paradox of choice."
The author reminds us: don't be a perfectionist "maximizer"; learn to be a "satisficer." When you meet someone suitable, generously give them a chance, rather than always keeping one foot out the door looking for someone better.

② Too independent → forgetting the need for intimacy
We work hard to achieve things for ourselves, to be free and easy, but we forget: "falling in love with someone" isn't a weakness, but a kind of energy.
Humans are social animals; romantic love is an emotional bond that friendship or family love can't replace.

║ If you want to find a partner, you must know these dating skills
1\.	Don't be a conversation killer (the king of full stops)

If someone asks about your work, don't just say "I teach"; you could say "I teach junior-high English, and right now I have a class of very dramatic eighth graders," leaving the conversation room to grow.

2\.	Keep the first date "lightweight"
Don't go to a fancy restaurant or an activity that runs too long. The date should be light, easy to extend or wrap up, like a café: 30 minutes to see how you feel about each other. If you click, extend it; if there's no spark, it's not awkward (just endure 30 minutes!).

3\.	Don't decide based on first impressions alone
Love isn't a job interview, nor a speed-matching data analysis.
Give the other person a second chance, and give yourself a little room for adventure. Many happy couples slowly fell in love later on.

║ Once the relationship is stable, these two things decide whether it lasts:
1\.	Handle "projected perfection"
Do you like the other person, or the person you imagine them to be? When they no longer match the fantasy, practice "accepting a real person," rather than always feeling love has gone bad.

2\.	Find the balance between "intimacy" and "independence"
Intimacy isn't being glued together every moment, but knowing how to give each other space.
Sometimes one simple sentence can repair a relationship: "I like you, but sometimes I get too clingy. If it makes you uncomfortable, please tell me."

║ Want to attract romantic prospects faster? It's not just about action; it's about "setting things up"!
Besides behavioral strategies, you can also bring in Eastern wisdom:
Zi Wei Dou Shu × feng shui space, so love comes faster and more naturally.

Feng shui is really a combination of spatial psychology and neuroscience.
Space affects how we feel, and also affects behavior; repeated behavior builds "neural pathways" in the brain, ultimately shaping your fate.

✽ How do you find your own peach blossom position?
Step 1: First locate the directions in your home
Open the "compass app" on your phone or check against your home's floor plan to find north, south, east and west.
The "Earthly Branch positions" in Zi Wei Dou Shu correspond to directions as follows:
•	Zi position ➝ due north
•	Wu position ➝ due south
•	Mao position ➝ due east
•	You position ➝ due west
You can map the Zi, Wu, Mao and You positions in your chart onto your home's floor plan and start looking for your peach blossom position.

✽ How to determine the peach blossom position (in order of priority)
1.	In the natal chart, the direction of the palace where Hong Luan sits (it can't be used if the palace has Sha stars or Hua Ji)
2.	The position of the Palace of Love and Partnership in the natal chart (no Sha stars or Hua Ji in the palace, ideally with a peach blossom star), or the direction where the Heavenly Stem of the Palace of Love and Partnership produces Hua Lu, or where Lu Cun is　　(but if that position has Sha stars or Hua Ji, it can't be used either)
3.	If none of the above can be used, look in this order:
　　- the ten-year Palace of Love and Partnership
　　- the annual Palace of Love and Partnership
4.	The palaces where the annual Hong Luan and Tian Xi sit (these differ each year, so change the position every year)
5.	Failing that, choose another palace in your chart that's in better shape
6.	Bonus: the palace's major star is a peach blossom star (such as the Seeker (Tan Lang), the Blessing Star (Tian Tong), and so on)
Note: if that direction happens to be a bathroom, kitchen, storeroom or similar space, it isn't suitable as a peach blossom position.

✽ Decorating the peach blossom position for the best effect
•	Put things you like there; red and pink are the best colors
•	You can place fresh flowers or orchids (avoid artificial flowers)
•	Candles, floor lamps and desk lamps can all serve as point light sources
•	Light incense at a fixed time every day to build a sense of ritual
•	Avoid sharp objects or knife and sword ornaments
•	Put a sheet of paper underneath, writing down a specific wish and action plan
　For example: "a more harmonious marriage," "attend 2 mixers a month," and so on

║ Fate and space were already written in your chart
On the 12 squares of a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart, each square is marked with its corresponding Earthly Branch direction. These branches symbolize not only time but also directions in space.
This is the spatial energy configuration you were born with, showing that in this life you're especially attuned to certain directions and settings. If you can make use of these directions and energies, together with concrete action, you'll naturally attract good romantic prospects into your life.
