Today I'm going to teach you the most "no-holds-barred" mind-reading technique in Zi Wei Dou Shu: Tai Sui ru gua.
No psychic powers needed.
All you need is the other person's "Chinese zodiac sign" + "your chart," and you can see
what they like about you, what they want to take from you, how they'll harm you, and how they can help you.

👉 It works in a huge range of situations:
someone you're flirting with → are they playing with you or sincere?
A colleague → are they undermining you or helping you?
Your boss → do they want to promote you, or use you?
A business partner → can you make money together, or will you go down together?
This technique is so accurate, please use it with care.

[The lesson begins] (Example: "wanting to know the other woman's influence")
1️⃣ Open your husband's chart.
2️⃣ Find the Earthly Branch position that corresponds to "the other woman's zodiac sign."
　For example: the other woman was born in the Year of the Snake → find the Si position in your husband's chart.
3️⃣ The "palace's Heavenly Stem" of this palace will cause stars in your husband's chart to transform.
4️⃣ The palaces where the stars' Four Transformations land show the areas in which the other woman influences your husband.

A brutal analysis: what gives that bitch the power to steal your beloved's heart?
(Example: the other woman was born in the Year of the Snake → the Si position → that palace's Heavenly Stem, Ding, projects the Four Transformations)
•	Hua Lu: the gentle haven that makes your husband happy
(the Moon (Tai Yin) with Hua Lu enters your husband's Palace of Inner Life and Fortune) The other woman's gentleness and sensitivity, her tenderness like water, directly soothe your husband's spirit and make him feel understood and nourished.
•	Hua Quan: the means by which she controls your husband
(the Blessing Star (Tian Tong) with Hua Quan enters the Palace of Inner Life and Fortune) She uses childlike behavior, acting cute and dependence to control your husband.

The Moon and the Blessing Star in the Palace of Inner Life and Fortune also mean your husband is very willing to take her out to enjoy herself, and happily spends money on her.
•	Hua Ke: making your husband feel proud
(the Strategist (Tian Ji) with Hua Ke enters your husband's Palace of the Outer World) Taking this other woman out makes him feel respectable and admired, satisfying his vanity.
•	Hua Ji: the point at which your husband can least do without her (the key)
(the Star of Obscurity (Ju Men) with Hua Ji enters your husband's Palace of Daily Focus) He's hopelessly hooked! Deep in his feelings, your husband feels he "can't live without her." This kind of attachment is the strongest, and the wife will have a very hard time competing!

[Interviewing employees]
Résumés can be faked and interviews can be acted, but energy can't lie.
As a boss, besides choosing people by their résumés, use this technique as a reference too, and your success rate in judging people will be very high.
✅ Looking for a salesperson (Hua Lu):
•	Lu flying into the boss's Palace of Money Management / Palace of Daily Focus: they can directly help you turn things into money, or give your work a huge boost.
✅ Looking for someone in public relations (Hua Ke):
•	Flying into the Palace of the Outer World / Palace of Friends and Associates: they can help you maintain your image, are socially adept with the outside world, and make the boss look very good.
❌ The palace of Hua Ji: where there's something good, there's bound to be something bad. The Four Transformations coexist, but you need to look at the role and the company's situation. If, for that role, the palace of Hua Ji causes losses, then even if the candidate's Hua Lu is in the Palace of Money Management!! don't hire them.
•	Hiring an accountant whose Ji flies into the boss's Palace of Money Management: they'll make you lose money.
