Every Valentine's Day, we tend to use rituals to keep love "fresh."
"Keeping something fresh means keeping it unchanged, but life can never stay unchanged, and neither can relationships."

The cruelest truth in real life is that, in the end, 95% of relationships don't last because the two people are still deeply in love, but because they slip into the inertia of "it's not bad enough to need ending."

Why do we always feel love is bound to fade? It isn't your fault; we're being worn down by three big killers:
1.	Hedonic adaptation: the brain gets used to stimulation, and hormones and passion naturally ebb.
2.	The illusion of too many choices: social media makes us feel there's always someone "better" available.
3.	A culture of faster replacement: modern society has trained us to "replace" a relationship with problems rather than "repair and resolve" it.

When one partner refuses to explore, because of the pressures of real life or because of their personality, your time together sinks entirely into "task-based togetherness": dates are for checking a box, gifts are for placating. Your partner is no longer someone who helps your life expand, but has regressed into a "functional partner" who splits the mortgage and the child-rearing.

When growth stops, the relationship starts to die.

║ The secret weapon against blandness: "deep inner activities"
Neuroscience has shown that some couples married for more than 20 years still have brains as active as in the early days of being madly in love. Deeper research found that this is because they don't treat love as "completing tasks"; they've mastered the mechanism that keeps love alive: continually rebuilding "us."

Through "deep inner activities," they take the relationship deeper. This isn't simply dealing with the chores of daily life together, but "creating meaning" together:
•	Not just eating to fill your stomach → but savoring, commenting and creating memories together.
•	Not just traveling so you can say "I spent time with you" → but reshaping together the way you see the world.

The point is to enjoy the process of being together itself, not to "hit the KPI of keeping each other company."

If asking your partner for a "deep conversation" right away is too hard, start with a "tiny incursion":
stop asking functional questions like "Tired today?",
try sharing something new you've discovered, invite them into a low-key shared experience,
and see whether the two of you can spark a "third perspective" that belongs to you both.

Zi Wei Dou Shu ║ Why is there always a "persona gap" in love?
Many people think that to read love, you only need to watch the Palace of Love and Partnership.
But in fact, to understand someone's truest state and conflicts in love, you need to layer the "Self–Outer World axis" (the Palace of Self and the Palace of the Outer World) and the "Partnership–Daily Focus axis" (the Palace of Love and Partnership and the Palace of Daily Focus) together (if you've studied Zi Wei Dou Shu, please bring in the Palace of Inner Life and Fortune too).

These four palaces are like different scripts we play in love (the following is based on the natal chart):
•	Palace of Love and Partnership: your values in love, the love you truly long for, and the kind of people you're easily drawn to.
•	Palace of Daily Focus (how love is shown): as the palace opposite the Palace of Love and Partnership, it represents your "subconscious thoughts" about the relationship, and "the state of your relationship you want to show outsiders."

•	The Self–Outer World axis (the underlying nature of a life): whatever you're reading, you can't leave out the Self–Outer World axis. The Palace of the Outer World determines your social mask, your social skills and your public image.

This is why we need to design "inner activities made for you":
when the stars in these palaces differ, it means your values as a person and your values in love are different.
We often attract people with the "social mask" of the Palace of the Outer World, or deal with our partner through the "armor" of the Palace of Daily Focus, while neglecting the soul in the Palace of Love and Partnership that truly needs to be soothed and understood.

✽ Want to invite your partner to reshape the relationship with you, but don't know where to start?
Understand how your partner's Palace of the Outer World (their public image, and also their inner self) combines with their Palace of Love and Partnership (their attitude toward love), and choose "inner activities" tailored to satisfy the qualities of these palaces at the same time. Only then can you treat the real issue precisely and bring the life back into your love. Today I'll pick five combinations of stars as examples:

1. [Palace of Love and Partnership: Treasurer (Wu Qu) group × Palace of the Outer World: Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen) group]: the endearing contrast of a social butterfly and an actuary
•	The contrast in love: outwardly a quick-witted, image-conscious social butterfly (the Star of Restraint, especially with peach blossom stars), but in a relationship an extremely practical actuary (the Treasurer). Outsiders think they want a whirlwind romance; in fact what they want is the security of "living well together and growing strong."
•	Inner activities made for them: [co-creating practical beauty] and [private debriefs after socializing]

•	High-level brainstorming: research the investment markets together or plan a challenging trip, and enjoy the flow of "taking a problem apart with your brains in sync."
•	Sharp tongues and heart-to-hearts: after a social gathering, drop the packaging and, with the Treasurer's bluntness, precisely dissect the day's people and events with your partner. This is your private space for rebuilding trust.

2. [Palace of Love and Partnership: Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen) group × Palace of the Outer World: Emperor (Zi Wei) group]: a soul collision between an aloof monarch and a wildly intense beauty
•	The contrast in love: outwardly extremely concerned with face, aloof and dignified (the Emperor); in a relationship, inwardly a sensitive, possessive, wildly intense talent or beauty (the Star of Restraint). Outsiders think they want a political marriage; in fact they want to burn in the fire.

•	Inner activities made for them: [making each other strangers again through soul-searching questions]
•	Leave your circle of acquaintances, go somewhere with a strong artistic feel, have a drink or two, and hold a "dangerous conversation" about human nature and your deepest fears. Breaking through the armor of dignity and seeing each other's most fragile and unhinged sides is what releases your own dopamine.

3. [Palace of Love and Partnership: Emperor (Zi Wei) group × Palace of the Outer World: Treasurer (Wu Qu) group]: an alliance of two sovereigns, hard-core warriors both
•	The contrast in love: outwardly a blunt, unsentimental money-making machine (the Treasurer), but inwardly drawn intensely to strength (the Emperor). They don't want an accessory who only knows how to act cute; they want a rival they can submit to, or one to rule beside them as an equal.

•	Inner activities made for them: [a shared plan of power and achievement]
•	The greatest romance is "conquering the kingdom together." Work through tough problems at work together, master a difficult professional field together. When the two of you show perfectly matched intelligence while taking ground, that is what keeps the relationship fresh.

4. [Palace of Love and Partnership: Guardian (Tian Liang) group × Palace of the Outer World: Moon (Tai Yin) group]: a symphony of old souls beneath a gentle shell
•	The contrast in love: outwardly gentle, reserved and in need of protection (the Moon), but in love longing to find (or become) a soul mentor who can shelter them from the storm and values conversations on a spiritual level (the Guardian).

•	Inner activities made for them: [an emotional archaeology that turns back time]
•	Avoid the noise; walk through old parts of the city together, make tea and listen to old records, or take a mind-body-spirit course. What you exchange isn't today's vegetable prices but your insights into life. This quiet, peaceful "being understood" releases the most stable oxytocin.

5. [Palace of Love and Partnership: Strategist (Tian Ji) group × Palace of the Outer World: Blessing Star (Tian Tong) group]: a brain-teasing maze for an optimist
•	The contrast in love: outwardly a lazy, easygoing, pressure-averse optimistic child (the Blessing Star), but in a relationship meticulous and prone to overthinking (the Strategist), placing great weight on a partner's "brain" and longing for clever back-and-forth.

•	Inner activities made for them: [light puzzle-solving]
•	Do a two-person escape room together, or take a road trip with no itinerary. This kind of "playful" brainwork soothes the Blessing Star's childlike heart and satisfies the Strategist's craving for a mental challenge.

Time will steal all passion, but it can't steal the "us" you keep rebuilding.
