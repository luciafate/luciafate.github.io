Having a paranormal experience takes three conditions: 1. the constitution, 2. the environment, 3. values that believe ghosts and gods exist.
If the Palace of Self, Palace of the Outer World, Palace of Inner Life and Fortune, Palace of Parents and Elders or Palace of Body and Health in your natal chart have the following stars, the more of them combine, the greater your chance of running into a ghost.
1\. Sha stars: they represent raw emotions. Emotions are mainly controlled by the brain's limbic system, which reacts faster than the prefrontal cortex that governs reason. "Intuition" is a fast cognitive judgment, often accompanied by an emotional reaction, and influenced by the limbic system's fast processing of outside stimuli such as sight, sound and smell, and we're usually "unaware" of it. When Sha stars appear in these palaces, your subconscious is especially sensitive to outside stimuli.
2\. Tian Wu, Hua Gai, Yin Sha: if a palace has these stars, paired with major stars connected to religion or that are highly changeable, then when the timing charts bring the right occasion, you're likely to have an affinity for religion and paranormal experiences.
3\. Major stars with an affinity for religion (the Seeker (Tan Lang), the Guardian (Tian Liang), the Star of Obscurity (Ju Men), the Blessing Star (Tian Tong)): more likely to come into contact with religion or spiritual things in life, which raises the chance of unusual experiences.
4\. Highly changeable stars (the Strategist (Tian Ji), the Moon (Tai Yin), the Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen)): the Strategist and the Moon are highly perceptive and easily affected; the Star of Restraint is a "five ghosts" star with a powerful energy field. When these stars are paired with Sha stars, paranormal experiences come easily.
The environment: when the timing charts overlap with or form hidden combinations with the following palaces, there's a chance of meeting a ghost: seeing ghosts when out (Palace of the Outer World, Palace of Children and Creation), meeting ghosts in the work environment (Partnership–Daily Focus axis), and if your home is a temple where rituals are performed (or a grave), the Children–Home axis is involved too!
