Everyone has insecurities; they just hide them differently.
Some go quiet and lock their emotions inside.
Some talk more, using liveliness as armor.
Some trade control for safety; some use effort to maintain a sense of presence.
But in the end, all insecurity comes down to one sentence: "Will I lose it? Am I good enough? Will you stop wanting me?"
In Zi Wei Dou Shu, this deep insecurity comes from Ju Men, the Star of Obscurity.

01 | Insecurity isn't "overthinking"; it's a psychological mechanism
"Insecurity" is a state of lacking inner stability and trust; at its core is the fear of loss, the fear of not being accepted, the fear of not being good enough.

It isn't simple worry but a deep psychological reaction that makes a person feel uncertain about themselves, relationships, their environment or the future, which then produces:
◎ oversensitivity and overthinking
◎ defensive reactions, checking again and again
◎ excessive control or anxiety
◎ perfectionism, people-pleasing tendencies

Insecurity isn't the state of only a few people; everyone may experience it at certain stages or in certain situations, and its depth and expression vary from person to person.

02 | Ju Men: the psychological black hole of insecurity
In Zi Wei Dou Shu, Ju Men is a dark star; its core quality is an (obscurity), symbolizing the black hole deep inside that the outside world doesn't see, and also "an unspoken but deeply hidden insecurity."

Ju Men's essence is staying highly alert to and suspicious of the outside world, using "thinking, analyzing, reasoning and eloquence" as ways of protecting itself.

It's good at running an inner simulation theater, playing out risks, and thinking up in advance... perhaps 20 worst-case endings!
Calm and rational on the outside, but racing at high speed inside.
The survival strategy Ju Men guards is:
"If I think through every bad possibility first, I won't get hurt."
So it's clever, sharp and observant,
but because of this, it gets very tired.

03 | Ju Men and the Sun: the black hole must see the light
Ju Men symbolizes darkness, so in a chart you also need to look at the position of the brightest light source, the Sun (Tai Yang).
✅ The Sun in a strong position (the Sun in the Earthly Branch positions Yin, Mao, Chen, Si, Wu, Wei)
→ Ju Men's insecurity is more latent
→ it may show up as late-night anxiety, or erupt in specific situations

✅ The Sun in a fallen position (the Sun in the Earthly Branch positions Shen, You, Xu, Hai, Zi, Chou)
→ Ju Men's insecurity is overt

04 | Common insecurities: everyone has at least one of these
① Insecurity about "yourself"
Core: am I good enough?
Behavior:
. caring too much about how others see you
. fearing being replaced, fearing not being needed
. trading achievement and performance for affirmation
→ In psychology: self-worth, attachment patterns

② Insecurity about "relationships" (most obvious when Ju Men falls in a relationship palace)
Core: will you leave me?
Behavior:
. worrying the other person's feelings will change even when nothing's wrong
. needing lots of replies and companionship
. letting your imagination run wild, checking again and again
→ Common in: anxious attachment, growing up with emotional deprivation

③ Insecurity about "the world and the future"
Core: I don't know what bad thing will happen, but I feel something always will
Behavior:
. over-controlling details, unable to relax

. especially anxious about money, health and work
. zoning out / doing nothing = panic; planning = security

05 | Ju Men's combinations
Ju Men shares a palace with or sits opposite only three stars.
Ju Men ↔ Tian Ji (opposite palace or same palace): clever and quick to react; insecurity shows through language, debate or a sharp tongue, more obviously with Sha stars (they talk very sharply!!)

Ju Men ↔ Tai Yang: turns insecurity into wanting to take care of others, wanting control, wanting to lead; driven by insecurity, they also study desperately to improve their abilities. When the Sun is in a strong position, this person is actually quite charismatic and has a strong voice.

Ju Men ↔ Tian Tong: easy to get along with and easygoing on the outside, afraid of being overlooked inside; in the Palace of Love and Partnership, meeting Sha stars or Hua Ji, behaviors like "keeping a backup partner, collecting emotional attention" easily appear.

Tian Tong + Ju Men in the same palace: big emotional swings, often struggling to get going or let go; they need themselves or the people close to them to keep topping up their sense of security with words or hugs.

06 | Ju Men's behavior patterns
Common patterns for Ju Men:
1. Ju Men, ruler of ranking things: because of insecurity, this easily turns into a habit of storing things away.
Hoarding → collecting both things and knowledge
(a chart with the Sun in a strong position + good fortunes = what's collected then tends to be valuable; a fallen Sun = prone to hoarding useless things)
2. Loves learning → because of the fear of "not knowing"
3. Lots of mental theater → over-simulating situations
4. Extremely sensitive to information → can read meaning in wording, tone and expressions

07 | The palace Ju Men falls in shows where you feel insecure
①	In a relationship palace (Love and Partnership / Parents and Elders / Siblings / Friends and Associates / Home and Property / Children and Creation)
= relationship-type insecurity

Common behaviors:
. checking phones, checking social media
. needing assurance and responses
. people-pleasing and accommodating
. running hot and cold, fearing being abandoned
. distance and coldness

②	In your own palaces (Self / Outer World / Daily Focus / Money Management / Inner Life and Fortune)
= self-worth-type insecurity
Common behaviors:
. using effort to maintain a sense of presence
. comparing too much, fearing losing, fearing not being good enough
. sense of achievement = sense of security
. anxious when not performing

✅ In one sentence: whichever palace holds Ju Men is the area where you're most anxious
Palace → source of anxiety
Palace of Love and Partnership → uncertainty in love and partnership
Palace of Daily Focus → anxiety about career and performance
Palace of Money Management → money, income, future risk
Palace of Inner Life and Fortune → inner worth, self-doubt
Palace of Self → all-round insecurity about the self, often disguised as strength

🔹 Insecurity isn't a defect, but a misunderstood gift
You can think of Ju Men as a "risk-detecting radar" in the heart. Because of past threats or a lack of stability, it keeps scanning for "possibilities that could hurt me," and even when the current environment is safe, the brain stays on guard!

But used correctly, this power becomes:
◎ deep insight
◎ sensitivity to emotion and language
◎ the ability to manage risk
◎ predictive thinking, the ability to plan ahead
◎ deep empathy

Please turn your insecurity into a strength.

Everyone has insecurities, and everyone's chart has Ju Men.
Which palace is your Ju Men in?
