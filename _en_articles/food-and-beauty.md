Pop quiz! Which palaces make up the Sanfang Sizheng of the Palace of Parents and Elders?

When it comes to the Sanfang Sizheng structure (the focal palace, its two trine palaces and its opposite palace), the set everyone knows best is probably "Self, Money, Daily Focus": the Palace of Self, the Palace of Money Management and the Palace of Daily Focus, plus the opposite palace, the Palace of the Outer World.

That set is so familiar, but it seems to be the only one people know. Switch to another palace and you have to stop and think XD

The answer: for the Palace of Parents and Elders, it's **the Palace of Children and Creation, the Palace of Friends and Associates, and the Palace of Body and Health.**

Many people don't realize that it isn't only the Palace of Self that has a Sanfang Sizheng. Every one of the twelve palaces has its own.

## Where does it come from?

Just take any palace and treat it as the "Palace of Self," treating it as the focal point (li tai ji).

With the Palace of Parents and Elders as the palace being read:

- Its opposite palace is the Palace of Body and Health, which represents how the Palace of Parents and Elders shows outwardly.
- Counting five palaces backward, the Palace of Children and Creation is the "Palace of Money Management" of the Palace of Parents and Elders.
- Counting five palaces forward, the Palace of Friends and Associates is its "Palace of Daily Focus," also called its qi shu wei.

Here's where it gets interesting.

In Zi Wei Dou Shu, the idea of the Palace of Money Management isn't only about money. When you set another palace as the center, its Money Management position also represents the resources that sustain that palace, its source of life.

And besides the familiar meanings of children, possessions and sexual life, the Palace of Children and Creation is also linked to food, drink and pleasure, and to taste in food.

And it just so happens that the Palace of Children and Creation is exactly the Money Management position of the Palace of Parents and Elders.

## The Parents–Body axis

The Palace of Body and Health relates to physical condition, illness, appearance, heredity, and how we use our bodies.

The Palace of Parents and Elders itself also carries the meaning of heredity, and as the palace opposite the Palace of Body and Health, it represents how your health, appearance and use of your body show outwardly.

So the axis of the Palace of Parents and Elders and the Palace of Body and Health is closely tied to the body, appearance and heredity to begin with.

Now open up the Sanfang Sizheng and an interesting structure appears:

- **Palace of Parents and Elders**: how others see your physical condition, appearance, heredity, and attitude toward using your body.
- **Palace of Children and Creation**: one of its meanings is exactly food, and it is also the Money Management position, the source of life, of the Palace of Parents and Elders.

And so body, appearance, health and diet all turn up in the same Sanfang Sizheng.

The point is, modern science has really found that what we eat over the long term gradually shows in how we look.

The traditional belief is that "eating less = a smaller face and better looks," but research indicates that an "unbalanced low-calorie diet" (picky eating or crash dieting) can, through a lack of protein and good fats disrupting sex hormones among other factors, actually push bone and soft tissue in a more masculine direction.

So!! What you eat really does affect how you look! Here are a few fascinating research papers.

## 1. Eat right, and your face shape may change?

**Balanced diet group**: the most defined face shape! Cheekbones of just the right width, clear contours with a natural, slightly sculpted look.

**High-calorie group**: more likely to become "round-faced"! The face gets wider, the midface recedes, and even the bridge of the nose looks lower and flatter.

**Crash-dieting or picky-eating group (low-calorie and unbalanced)**: the most counterintuitive! Lacking good protein and vitamins (D, B12, n-3) disrupts hormones, making the face longer and the eyes look smaller, with features even tending toward the masculine!

*(Tanikawa et al., 2022)*

## 2. A sugary drink a day, and your face slowly sags

The "glycation" brought on by a high-sugar diet does serious damage to skin elasticity. A sweet drink every day is the hidden culprit that, over time, leads your skin toward sagging!

*(Gkogkolou & Böhm, 2012)*

## 3. Want a natural healthy glow? Eating more fruit and vegetables works fast

- After eating more fruit and vegetables for **2 weeks**, instruments can already measure changes in the skin; after **4–6 weeks**, the redness and yellow tones of the face increase noticeably!
- With enough beta-carotene, your complexion looks healthier, and your overall attractiveness gets a big boost too!

*(Whitehead et al., 2012; Foo et al., 2017)*

## 4. The softer the food, the more your chin may recede

**Chewing stimulus and bone development**: eating soft, processed food over long periods during the growth years, with too little mechanical chewing stimulus, may lead to underdeveloped jaws, a receding chin, crowded teeth and a longer face!

## Conclusion: You are what you eat!

If you want to look better, start with what you eat at today's meal~

"Diet affects appearance" may work through nutrient intake, body composition, metabolic state, and even chewing stimulus during the growth years, gradually showing in the skin, the soft tissue of the face, or the shape of the bones.

On this topic, two different systems of knowledge make for an interesting, and easy to remember, comparison.

The twelve palaces of Zi Wei Dou Shu were never twelve separate labels.

The Palace of Children and Creation isn't only about children. The Palace of Parents and Elders isn't only about your father. The Palace of Body and Health isn't only about getting sick.

Every palace has multiple meanings, and each moves together with its own Sanfang Sizheng.

Once you start opening up the Sanfang Sizheng of each palace, many meanings that once made you wonder, "Why would this palace stand for that?", will gradually connect.
