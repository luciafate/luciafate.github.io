"Mu Gua" in the Airs of Wei, from the Book of Songs, has a famous line:
"You threw me a quince; I gave you a fine jade in return. Not as repayment, but so that we might be close forever."
Roughly: you tossed me a quince, and I give you a piece of beautiful jade in return.
This isn't simply returning a gift; it's that I want things to stay good between us for a long, long time.
Wait.
"Throwing a quince?" What does that mean? (The first time I read this line I thought: is this hitting someone?)
This was a charming courtship custom of the Book of Songs era:
when a woman saw a man she liked, she would throw the fruit she had on hand at him.
Not an attack, but a declaration of love.

If the man felt the same way, he would give one of his own tokens in return as a reply.
So "Mu Gua" in the Airs of Wei isn't writing about ordinary exchange of courtesies.
It's writing about an exchange of hearts.

You threw a quince to me. I didn't play dumb, or turn and walk away.
I answered you with fine jade. I'm telling you:
I understand what you mean, and I'm willing to let this feeling carry on.
That's how the ancients flirted.

No dating apps. No being left on read. No testing the waters with stories.
One quince was enough to run the test "I like you; will you respond?"
Shift the scene through time.
Then they threw quinces; now we throw messages.
Then they waited for a jade pendant in return; now we wait for a reply: "I want to see you too."
Thousands of years have passed, and in essence human romance hasn't changed much.
Only the setting and the tools have changed.
But then the question arises:
why do some people clearly have peach blossom luck, yet never manage to get into a relationship?

Why do some people get pursued, yet feel nothing at all?
Why do some relationships start with butterflies, yet end up draining?

From the perspective of Zi Wei Dou Shu, romance isn't only about "whether you have peach blossom stars."
At least five things need to be discussed.

First, do you really want to be in a relationship?
When people ask about love, the most common question is: "Teacher, when will I have peach blossom luck?"
But the more fundamental question is: do you really want to be in a relationship right now??

Some people don't lack peach blossom luck; at this stage they simply haven't made room for love in their lives.
Their mouths say they want to find someone, but their bodies are very honest:
straight home after work,
curled up on the sofa scrolling on their phone on weekends,
turning down friends' invitations whenever they can,
tired of dating apps after a couple of swipes,
and when someone comes close, the first thought is: forget it, too much trouble.
In this state, even if a "quince" is thrown your way, you won't necessarily catch it.

In Zi Wei Dou Shu,
whether you want to be in a relationship can first be seen by whether the timing-cycle Palace of Self overlaps the Palace of Love and Partnership.
The palace the timing-cycle Palace of Self overlaps represents "what I care about during this period."
If the timing-cycle Palace of Self overlaps the natal or ten-year Palace of Love and Partnership, it means that during this period, love more easily enters your field of attention.

Another key point is whether any star in the timing-cycle Palace of Love and Partnership has Hua Ji,
especially a peach blossom star with Hua Ji.
The Palace of Love and Partnership is about the state of your love life to begin with;
a star with Hua Ji stands for a gap, longing, caring, being unable to let go.
So when a star in the timing-cycle Palace of Love and Partnership has Hua Ji, especially a peach blossom star, the sense of emptiness and the need for love become pronounced.
You may start to feel:

as if someone is missing,
I really want to be loved,
I really want company,
I want to be understood,
I don't want to deal with everything alone anymore.

That's when the thought of being in a relationship gets drawn out.
So the first hurdle in romance isn't whether there's someone,
but whether the door of your heart is open,
whether you really want to catch that "quince."

Second, do you have the qualities that make people like you?
Wanting a relationship is one thing.
Having the qualities that make people like you is another.
To see whether someone is attractive, gets noticed and has the qualities to be liked, you can't look only at the Palace of Love and Partnership.
You need to look at the Palace of Self and the Palace of the Outer World, and the Palace of Love and Partnership and the Palace of Daily Focus.
The Palace of Self: your innate personality traits.
The Palace of the Outer World: the impression others have of you, and your inner thoughts.
The Palace of Love and Partnership: your view of love, and the kind of people you tend to attract in romantic interaction.
The Palace of Daily Focus: besides how you get things done day to day and your performance at work, it's also how others see the state of your love life, and the deep inner thoughts behind your own view of love. The timing-cycle Palace of Daily Focus can also represent your partner.

If the Self–Outer World and Partnership–Daily Focus axes have peach blossom stars,
such as the Seeker (Tan Lang), the Blessing Star (Tian Tong), the Moon (Tai Yin), the Keeper of the Seal (Tian Xiang), the Star of Obscurity (Ju Men), the Star of Depletion (Po Jun), Wen Qu, Hong Luan or Tian Xi,
it means this person more readily has qualities that get them noticed, make people like them, and make people want to come close.

It isn't necessarily only about being beautiful or handsome.
The attraction of peach blossom comes in many kinds:
some are wonderful conversationalists who understand you,
some are gentle,
some are talented,
some have a cool, slightly distant aura,
some make people feel comfortable,
or are very good at looking after emotions,
or naturally carry an atmosphere that makes people want to come close.

The woman in the Book of Songs who throws the fruit shows that she first saw, noticed and fell for that man.
It's the same for people today.
The other person has to notice you first before they have a chance to warm to you.
So the second hurdle is:
do you have the conditions to be seen?
Do you have qualities that make people warm to you?
Are you in a state that makes others willing to come close?

Third, is there extra romantic opportunity during this period?
You want a relationship, and you have the qualities that make people like you; you also need to see whether romantic opportunities are increasing during this period.
For this, look at the timing-cycle Palace of Love and Partnership.
If a star in the timing-cycle Palace of Love and Partnership has Hua Lu, or there's Lu Cun,
it means romantic opportunities easily increase during this period.
Hua Lu means something extra:
extra goodwill,
extra interaction,
extra flirtation,
extra romantic possibilities.
If a peach blossom star with Hua Lu sits in the Palace of Love and Partnership, the romantic opportunities are even more obvious.

Because peach blossom stars are attractive to begin with, and Hua Lu boosts that attractive quality,
it's easier for people to come close to you, take an interest in you, and want more interaction with you.

But let me be clear here:
having Hua Lu doesn't mean you'll definitely meet "the right one";
having peach blossom luck doesn't mean you'll definitely get together;
someone coming close doesn't mean that person is definitely right for you.
Hua Lu is more like "more opportunities."
As for what kind of people come once the opportunities increase, and how you choose, that's the more important question.

Fourth, the environment has to cooperate for peach blossom to have a place to happen
Only now do we return to the Book of Songs.
The love poems in the Book of Songs are very interesting.

A lot of romance happens by the river, at festivals, on spring outings, in singing back and forth, while gathering, on the road of daily work.
For example, "Zhen Wei" in the Airs of Zheng describes the Shangsi Festival, when young men and women go out to the banks of the Zhen and Wei rivers for a spring outing, playing games together and giving each other peonies.
To put it plainly, that's the ancient version of a big outdoor mixer.
With a setting, there can be an encounter;
with an encounter, there can be interaction;
with interaction, there can be an exchange of hearts.

Even the ancients understood:
for love to happen, you first have to show up in places where love can happen.
If you don't go out, there's no playing by the river;
if you don't go to gatherings, there's no singing back and forth;
if you don't meet people, no one will throw you a quince.

It's the same today.
If every day you go only between the office and home, don't go out on weekends, haven't refreshed your circle of friends in ten years, and there's no potential partner at the office, then even if your chart is full of peach blossom stars, it's very hard for anything to happen in real life.

In addition, Zi Wei Dou Shu can show where your romantic connections may come from.
Look at which palace the annual Palace of Love and Partnership overlaps, and you can roughly infer the setting for romance.
Overlapping the Palace of Friends and Associates / Palace of Daily Focus: perhaps someone from work, or a colleague playing matchmaker.
Overlapping the Palace of Money Management / Palace of Daily Focus: perhaps meeting through business dealings, finance or partnerships.
Overlapping the Palace of Friends and Associates / Palace of Siblings: perhaps an introduction by friends or siblings.
Overlapping the Palace of Home and Property: perhaps arranged by relatives in the family.
Overlapping the Palace of Inner Life and Fortune or the Palace of Children and Creation: perhaps meeting while out having fun, at a course, a gathering, an event, eating, drinking and playing, or in leisure settings.
The chart can remind you which kinds of settings to show up in more in real life,
where your chances of meeting a suitable partner are higher.

Fifth, once your heart is moved, you still need to see whether the other person is right for you
OK, now suppose all the conditions above are met:
you want a relationship,
you have the qualities that make people like you,
romantic opportunities are increasing during this period,
and you've actually walked into settings where you can meet people.

The quince has been thrown, your heart is moved, and then what?
Now you need to look at whether the person who appears is right for you.

In Zi Wei Dou Shu, if the timing-cycle Palace of Love and Partnership has too many Sha stars and Hua Ji, be careful.
It doesn't mean you'll meet a bad person, only that during this period it's more likely that
the wrong person appears;
you know something's off, yet you're still drawn in;
the relationship has lots of problems, yet it's hard to pull out;
the other person runs hot and cold, and you sink deeper;
a little sweetness makes you forget a lot of discomfort.

Especially if the timing-cycle Palace of Love and Partnership has Tuo Luo, it's even easier to misjudge people and pick the wrong partner.
Tuo Luo carries a state of holding back, entanglement, spinning in place, indecision, being hard to cut off.
In love, this easily means
misjudging people, picking the wrong partner, knowing something's wrong yet still being unable to let go.

What is most moving about "Mu Gua" in the Airs of Wei has never been the quince, but that someone caught it.
Peach blossom lets people meet.
But for a relationship to happen, it isn't enough for one side to send out a signal, nor for one person to wait in vain; it takes someone offering their heart, and someone willing to respond.
So don't only ask: why doesn't anyone like me?
Also ask:
does the door of my heart really want to open?
Have I walked into places where love can happen?
Am I able to see clearly the people who come?

You threw me a quince; I return you fine jade.
Not just a flirtation, but so that we might be close forever.
