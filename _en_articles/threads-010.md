Scrolling on my phone after work, I saw another news story about sudden death among young and middle-aged adults, and I hesitated a bit over whether to write about this topic.
Because when it comes to health, Zi Wei Dou Shu can only serve as a reminder: a reminder to get health checkups and to watch certain parts of the body.
But if it can remind people in advance, perhaps some regrets won't happen.

Many sudden deaths in young and middle-aged adults aren't actually "sudden."
More often, long-term risks build up to a tipping point, and the body can't hold out any longer.

Medically, the common causes are, by and large:
cardiovascular problems, cerebrovascular problems, arrhythmia, and the chain reactions caused by overwork, long-term poor sleep and chronic stress.

Zi Wei Dou Shu can show which parts of your body are more prone to problems, and when they may be triggered.

One general principle for reading a chart:
whether in the natal chart or the timing charts, the Parents–Body axis is best kept clean, with no Sha stars and no transformations.

Sha stars or transformations on the Parents–Body axis of the natal chart don't necessarily mean you will get ill, but may indicate inherited problems.

The timing charts match reality closely: people age, and the body accumulates.
How you've slept, eaten and worked over these years, how you've suppressed your emotions, how you've worn yourself out, all gets etched into the body bit by bit, and shows up in the chart too.

So if you find Sha stars, Hua Ji or transformations appearing again and again on the Parents–Body axis in every ten-year cycle, the reminder is:
please be sure to get regular health checkups.
Especially if the person is already middle-aged or older, this must not be ignored.

For sudden deaths of this kind among young and middle-aged adults, if the cause is a heart or cerebrovascular problem: the Earthly Branch positions Chen and You represent the chest, and Wu and Wei represent the head.
When Sha stars, Hua Ji or transformations concentrate in these positions, please also remind the person to pay extra attention to the heart and brain and get them checked.
Daily routine, stress, diet and exercise need to be adjusted at the same time.

This year, the annual Tuo Luo is in the Chen position, and Qing Yang is in the Wu position.
In this Bing Wu year, especially for friends with Lian Zhen–Tian Fu and Qi Sha sitting in Chen and Xu, if you were also born in a year ending in 6 in the Western calendar, that is, a Bing year, this axis will be heavily loaded with Sha stars and Hua Ji, and there's also a lot of Qing Yang in the Wu position. Watch your health!

Don't neglect your body for years for the sake of career, responsibilities, family or making a living, putting yourself last.
Without health, nothing else is real.

Tian Ji, Wen Chang and Wen Qu all fear Sha stars and Hua Ji, so thinking patterns and mental health need attention. Get checked when you should.
Rest when you should.
Adjust your routine, diet, exercise and stress when you should, and really do it.
Zi Wei Dou Shu can flag risks.
But what can truly save you is whether you're willing to face your physical condition early and make changes.
