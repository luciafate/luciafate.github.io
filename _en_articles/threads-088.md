║ Zi Wei Dou Shu
The 14 major stars have different tendencies in recovery. Which kind of self-healing is yours?

The Sun (Tai Yang) | Returning to rhythm and responsibility
Pain point: self-blame, or wanting to discipline the world into "how it should be"
First aid: don't numb the pain with control and over-giving. Take a 20-minute walk in daylight, and write down "what I can control & what I can't"
Long term: turn your energy into service (a bigger love) but set boundaries (a cap of 1 hour a week); don't fall into rescuing others to escape yourself

The Treasurer (Wu Qu) | Steadying the heart with an SOP
Pain point: don't just act without speaking; your emotions need an outlet
First aid: tell a trusted friend how you feel, and break recovery into small tasks: sleep / move / eat / read / socialize
Long term: regularly take stock of your finances and job skills, so a sense of groundedness flows back; don't numb yourself with overtime

The Blessing Star (Tian Tong) | Only being loved can replenish it
Pain point: longing to be comforted, prone to emotional dependence
First aid: find family or a safe person to hug, say what you need, and plan "easy little joys"
Long term: practice "dates with yourself" to become emotionally self-sufficient; don't keep yourself going on sweets, alcohol or bad romances

The Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen) | Living better is the best revenge
Pain point: wounded self-esteem, impulsive testing or revenge
First aid: make no relationship decisions for 72 hours; only take care of your body and emotions
Long term: talk deeply with someone you trust, write down "I actually really care about __," and put your emotions into creative work or exercise; don't pretend on social media that nothing happened

When your heart is broken, real healing isn't "forgetting quickly," but slowly letting yourself become someone who can feel and live again.
