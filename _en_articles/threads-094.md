1\. The latest research: AI is reshaping the labor market
A Stanford team's study, "Canaries in the Coal Mine?",
used 25 million real payroll records from ADP, compared them against how exposed different occupations are to AI, and laid out six key facts:

1.	Young people aged 22–25 saw the clearest decline in employment in highly AI-exposed occupations.
2.	Employment of middle-aged and older workers aged 35–49 actually rose.
3.	The number of jobs fell, but wage levels didn't drop significantly (sticky wages + the remaining positions are worth more).
4.	The impact of AI only appeared from late 2022; it isn't a long-accumulating trend.
5.	This isn't a special case of one industry or company, but a widespread phenomenon across industries.
6.	The key difference lies in "how AI is used":
o	Automation → replaces human labor, cuts jobs
o	Augmentation → amplifies human labor, creates opportunities

Conclusion: the impact of AI isn't evenly spread; it hits young new graduates hardest. The real watershed is whether you "use AI mindlessly (automation)" or "collaborate with it (augmentation)."

2\. Two routes for using AI:
1\. Automation
Handing work entirely to AI, with humans just giving instructions

Examples:
Letting AI write a complete program and putting it straight into production.
Letting AI generate a report and handing it straight to your boss.
Letting AI take over customer service and handle all the replies.
The problem: these tasks are highly standardized and the easiest to replace.

2\. Augmentation
AI as a "brainpower plug-in," doing basic processing or giving you options, while the final judgment, integration and creativity are still yours.

Three typical modes:
①	Iterative task-oriented
Humans and AI interact back and forth, making the results better and better.
Suits people whose Zi Wei Dou Shu major stars are "perfectionist and patient."

② Learning-oriented
AI as a teacher, helping you absorb new knowledge quickly.
Suits people whose major stars are "widely learned, proactive in learning, curious."

③ Verification-oriented
AI helps you check and verify your results.
Suits people who are "rational, check from multiple angles, afraid of making mistakes."
