║ Zi Wei Dou Shu
Zi Wei Dou Shu is the last of the fortune-telling arts in Chinese culture to reach maturity.
Over the course of history it absorbed ideas from Indian astrology, the I Ching, Ba Zi, Qi Men Dun Jia and even ancient Chinese medicine, evolving step by step into today's complete, deep and practical system.

║ What Zi Wei Dou Shu is used for
Reading the stars together with the palaces lets you understand the different sides of your personality and find your inborn strengths and weaknesses. Add the timing charts, bringing in changes of time and space, and you can check the past and predict the future, helping you seek the good, avoid the bad, and prepare in advance.

Many people are skeptical of fortune-telling and feel it's just superstition or mysticism with no basis.
But in fact, disciplines grounded in human experience, like psychology and behavioral science, were also hard to verify repeatedly in their early days through rigorous experiments of the kind used in physics or chemistry. (Of course, these social sciences still have their own ways of scientific verification.) But what I want to stress is this: "can't be fully verified by science" doesn't mean "has no practical use."

Imagine if there were a method that could help you get to know yourself more systematically,
and even predict the "likelihood" of events happening in particular situations,
just as a financial model can help you forecast stock trends and raise your chances of success.
Would you be willing to give yourself a chance to understand it?

Zi Wei Dou Shu is like an ancient data-science system. Through more than a thousand years of observation and induction, it built a model that can predict a person's "behavioral tendencies" at a particular time and in a particular environment.
If you're willing, this series will walk you through it step by step.
