2026 is almost here, and the year's star configurations aren't favorable: the Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen) with Hua Ji, and the annual Palace of Self carrying Qing Yang: desire going too far, gray-area methods, shortcuts running rampant, killing without blinking!
In a sentence: next year, the outside environment may push the "index of human nature turning dark" to a record high.
Confucius taught us: the noble person understands what is right; the petty person understands what is profitable.
But in today's workplace, you'll find the real-life version is:
"Those who value loyalty die fast; those who value profit rise fast."

1\. The real reason backstabbers are hard to spot
At first, they're all good to you:
backstabbers often come wrapped in "friendliness, concern and offers to help," getting you to lower your guard.
You think you've met a benefactor, but in fact you've met your gravedigger.

2\. Three rules for recognizing backstabbers
1.	Look at "the direction of benefit"
A kind person starts from your "greatest long-term interest."
A backstabber uses your "short-term weaknesses" to give you small gains, while damaging your fundamental interests.
It looks like a good thing; only when you see the whole picture do you realize it's a trap.
2.	Look at "how they treat others"
Don't just look at whether they're good to you; also look at whether they're mean or jealous toward others.
If they're nasty to others and only temporarily good to you, they'll turn on you once you lose your standing.
3.	Look at "the people around them"
Birds of a feather flock together.
The circles a person moves in reflect their values and way of doing things.

3\. Three principles for guarding against backstabbers
1\. Give up greed
Those harmed by backstabbers are mostly greedy themselves.
Wanting a bargain, wanting a shortcut, wanting to avoid detours, and falling straight into the pit.
By the same token, if you don't entertain crooked thoughts, you needn't fear backstabbers.

2\. Keep your distance
Keep your distance from closeness that is overly warm "when they need something from you."
When you have money and power, your door is crowded with friends; when you lose it, the doorway goes quiet.

3\. Keep business strictly business
Don't act on the illusion that "I thought we got along well."
Make things clear before signing a contract; say the unpleasant things up front before money is involved.
Give what should be given; guard against what should be guarded against.
Respecting the rules is the most practical mercy toward human nature.

Finally, here's a dark little feng shui trick for you:
to make backstabbers back off, no need to quarrel, no need to fight, no need to burn talismans.
Give them a cute-looking potted plant: a succulent, a cactus or an agave will all do.
Soothing on the outside, actually carrying sha.
Thorny or pointed plant shapes bring negative energy, make fortune turn bad, and are bad for career development!

In fact, the most effective revenge is letting them slowly panic in your calm!!
