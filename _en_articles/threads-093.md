[What 25 million real records tell us: who is most at risk in the AI era! How can the 14 major stars of Zi Wei Dou Shu use AI to turn things around?]
Three typical modes of AI augmentation:
① Iterative task-oriented
Humans and AI interact back and forth, making the results better and better
② Learning-oriented
AI as a teacher, helping you absorb new knowledge quickly
③ Verification-oriented
AI helps you check and verify your results

║ The 14 major stars of Zi Wei Dou Shu × modes of AI augmentation
Your Zi Wei Dou Shu chart is determined by your time of birth: the talents and abilities you carry for this whole life. Look at your chart, at the major stars in your Palace of Self, Palace of Daily Focus and Palace of Inner Life and Fortune, and you can find "the most comfortable way for you to use AI":
The Emperor (Zi Wei) suits [iterative, verification]: cares about face, wants to control the game, afraid of mistakes; when decisions are involved, + [learning] to fill in sources of information

The Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen) suits [iterative, verification]: values image, doesn't want to make mistakes; when diplomacy / communication is needed, + [learning] to take in perspectives

The Treasurer (Wu Qu) suits [verification]: practical, values data and real benefits; can mix in [iterative] to make SOPs and spreadsheets more complete

The Resolute Star (Qi Sha) [iterative, verification]: dares to charge ahead, first-rate execution, will endure anything for a goal; its personality is sometimes too impatient for the learning mode

The Star of Depletion (Po Jun) [iterative, verification]: born creative + loves tearing things down and starting over; recommended to combine all three, adding + [learning] to avoid charging around blindly

The Seeker (Tan Lang) [learning]: very curious, loves learning, wants to experience everything, experiments quickly with creative ideas; can mix in [iterative]

The Chancellor (Tian Fu) [iterative, verification]: steady, wants everything under its control; when it needs to collect benchmark cases, can mix in [learning]

The Keeper of the Seal (Tian Xiang) [iterative, verification]: values norms, wants to be presentable, matches people, things and matters; when coordinating, can mix in [learning] to fill in background knowledge

The Strategist (Tian Ji) [learning + iterative]: the thinking type, loves learning; verification is usually needed when reviewing

The Guardian (Tian Liang) [learning]: likes adding experience and wisdom → AI as a database; can mix in [verification] to check for ethical risks

The Blessing Star (Tian Tong) [learning]: likes easygoing learning → AI as a simplifying teacher; can mix in [iterative] to help polish ideas

The Sun (Tai Yang) [iterative, verification]: wants to lead, wants status → iterate on copy + verify accuracy; can mix in [learning] to add perspectives that shine more

The Moon (Tai Yin) [verification]: meticulous, afraid of mistakes → likes checking; can mix in [learning] to learn gentle communication phrasing

The Star of Obscurity (Ju Men) [iterative, learning]: the anxious type, always wanting to know a bit more → learning + iterating; before going public, can mix in [verification] to check its arguments

Basically:
the perfectionist, afraid-of-mistakes type → leans toward "iterative + verification"
the curious, happy-to-learn type → leans toward "learning"
the meticulous type → leans toward "verification"

AI isn't simply an enemy; it's an amplifier.
Choose automation → you may be replaced.
Choose augmentation → you can leverage it and amplify your results.

Zi Wei Dou Shu helps you see clearly your most natural mode of using AI. Once you find the right way, AI isn't here to take your job, but to help you close the gap with more experienced people, and even overtake them.
