The kind of person you've surely met, whose "face you have to save"
Are there people like this around you?
1\. At work, the person who doesn't speak loudly, yet brings an aura with them the moment they walk into a meeting room?
They haven't even lost their temper, yet everyone automatically makes way and waits for them to decide.

2\. In everyday life, the friend with poise, who really knows how to enjoy things and has taste?
Particular about food, tasteful in dress, generous with money, knows how to eat, drink and have fun, raised very well by their family from childhood.

3\. In the family, the person you "can't speak too bluntly to," whose "face you must save"?
Mild on the surface, but in fact with strong self-esteem and very concerned about face, steering where everyone's decisions go.

If you just thought of someone,
congratulations, you already understand half of the Emperor star.

In Zi Wei Dou Shu, a star is a human trait.
So what kind of personality does the Emperor (Zi Wei) represent?

1\. Overview of the Emperor star
Core quality: zun (dignity) (the emperor star, ruler of the Palace of Daily Focus, its element is Yin Earth)
Core spirit:
not simply vanity, but "wanting face, wanting status, wanting to be admired, wanting a stage."
Key point: "Whichever palace the Emperor sits in is the area the chart owner cares about and can't lose face in."

2\. The Emperor star: personality and mindset
║ Personality and mindset
– Cares about dignity, image and quality; has taste and class.
– Likes to be revered and cheered on: "Everyone's attention should be on me."
– Easily swayed, loves to hear nice things, and relies heavily on a "team of ministers" (an emperor can't do everything personally; to be a leader who is followed, someone has to follow).

– Willing to quietly work hard and build real strength for the sake of face, wanting to be worthy of the position; but once it feels ignored or looked down on, it turns hostile very easily.
In a sentence: the Emperor isn't arrogant; it "can't afford to lose its dignity."

║ The palace = where its self-esteem lies
Wherever you place the Emperor is like putting "someone with very strong self-esteem" into that room:
– The Emperor in the Palace of Self: how could I lose in life?
– The Emperor in the Palace of Love and Partnership: I can't lose face in love.
– The Emperor in the Palace of Money Management: spending must look decent, must be name brands.
– The Emperor in the Palace of Home and Property: criticizing their family = stepping straight on a landmine.
The palace where the Emperor sits is their "landmine of self-esteem."

║ The Emperor on the high platform: abilities that match, and a grand scale
You'll see traits like these:
– Good taste, stands by people, steady in getting things done.
– Knows how to manage people and work with a team (an emperor has always had to rely on his ministers).
– Particular about spending, with a style of its own.
– Easily becomes the central figure.
This kind of Emperor is "an emperor in name and in fact."

║ The emperor without ability: outsized ambitions, abilities still developing
– Princess / prince syndrome: precious about its body, afraid of tiring itself, afraid of hassle.
– Keeping up appearances beyond its means: spending recklessly for the sake of face.
– Too much self-esteem: flares up the moment it's ignored.
– Feels "the whole world has wronged me."

║ A reminder: the high platform and the abyss
– The emperor with both a team and ability:
emperors of old called themselves "gu" and "gua ren" (the lonely one, the one of little virtue), reminding themselves to step down from the high platform at the right times, go to the scene in person, and not drift too far from their ministers and people. How will you find the balance between the high platform and people's hearts?

– The emperor with too many Sha stars and no ability:
when you care only about your own feelings, only about face, once your fortune takes a bad turn, it's very easy to be pushed into the abyss.

3\. The Emperor in daily life:
║ Work:
It wants to be the central figure; its real abilities must keep up, and it should pay special attention to getting along with people and the team.
║ Wealth:
Spending tends to run high, particular about taste and brands. It needs to assess whether its finances can really support its image, or it becomes "face wins, savings lose everything."
║ Love:
It wants a partner with great credentials and good status, while also hoping to be treasured.
It likes "the best of the best," and also wants the partner to wait on it in the relationship; in reality, it's hard to have both.

4\. Jun chen qing hui (the gathering of ruler and ministers): the Emperor's throne is held up by its team
If the Emperor has these stars around it, it's like a tiger given wings:
in the san fang si zheng (the palace itself, its opposite palace, and the two palaces in trine) of the palace where the Emperor sits, there are
Zuo Fu, You Bi, Tian Kui, Tian Yue, the Chancellor (Tian Fu) and the Keeper of the Seal (Tian Xiang).
The more of them, the better.
Without its ministers, the chart owner easily feels:
"so lonely, so hard to get things done, no one following, I have to carry everything myself."

5\. Five Emperor double-star combinations: five emperors' lives
║ Zi Wei–Tian Fu (the Emperor with the Chancellor) in the Yin and Shen positions, with Qi Sha (the Resolute Star) opposite
zun × resources × authority
– Very capable, on a grand scale, knows how to enjoy and how to plan: an emperor who "wants face and has substance too."
– With Sha stars, or without a team: easily becomes snobbish, haughty, fond of ostentation, without the real strength to back it up.
– The remedy: first get clear on "right now, do I want money or power? Do I want face, or what's practical?"

║ Zi Wei–Tian Xiang (the Emperor with the Keeper of the Seal) in the Chen and Xu positions, with Po Jun (the Star of Depletion) opposite
zun × order × dreams

– Mild, organized and well-connected on the surface, but in fact very much its own person, unwilling to be ordinary.
– Good at operating within the system, skilled at coordinating and playing a key role.
– With Sha stars and Hua Ji: prone to playing in gray areas, unorthodox paths, legal trouble and speculation.
– The remedy: don't sacrifice principles for goals; watch out for "the consequences of playing with fire."

║ Zi Wei–Qi Sha (the Emperor with the Resolute Star) in the Si and Hai positions, with Tian Fu (the Chancellor) opposite
zun × will × fighting power
– Capable of carrying things out personally; one of the few combinations that can "not rely much on a team."
– Has vision and dares to charge ahead, willing to endure hardship, able to bear the load: an emperor "able in both letters and arms."
– With Sha stars, or the Emperor with Hua Quan: prone to being overbearing, managing with high pressure, ignoring risks and people's feelings, wanting only to win.
– The remedy: after reaching the goal, be sure to turn back, tidy up, tend to relationships and deal with the aftermath.

║ Zi Wei–Po Jun (the Emperor with the Star of Depletion) in the Chou and Wei positions, with Tian Xiang (the Keeper of the Seal) opposite
zun × transformation × tact
– Energetic, charming, open-handed; with tactful skill, it wants to reform and pioneer, and dares to tear down old systems.
– May have short-lived enthusiasm and love grand achievements; its abilities can't support its dreams, and it comes and goes quickly.
– The remedy: first calculate the risks and resources, then decide whether to "blow it up and start over."

║ Zi Wei–Tan Lang (the Emperor with the Seeker) in the same palace in the Mao and You positions, or facing each other across the Zi–Wu axis
zun × desire × peach blossom
– Clever and widely learned, popular, loves to try things, values experiences and inner feelings.
– Easily led by pleasure, peach blossom and interests; with Sha stars and Hua Ji, it may become absorbed in sensory pleasures, and could even go too far into religion.
– The remedy: remind yourself "what the main thread is." Enjoyment is fine, but don't let desire lead the emperor by the nose.
