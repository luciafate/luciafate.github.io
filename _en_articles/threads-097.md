① Common sense: see the behavioral traits clearly first
⚠️ Before getting into a relationship, first watch whether they show these 5 major signs:
1.	A strong need to control and possess (checking up on you, restricting your friendships).
2.	Unable to communicate (you can only obey; there's no room for discussion).
3.	Violence or cold violence (insults, throwing things, emotional blackmail).
4.	Easily explosive emotions (flaring up over small things).
5.	Heavy suspicion (every person of the opposite sex is suspect).
→ If two or more appear, draw a red line first.

② Checking the chart: high-risk star combinations
Look at 5 main palaces: the Palace of Self, Palace of Love and Partnership, Palace of Inner Life and Fortune, Palace of Home and Property (domestic violence), and Palace of Body and Health.
If these palaces hold strong-willed stars or emotional stars, that shows inborn personality and ways of responding.

High-risk combinations:
•	The Resolute Star (Qi Sha) in the Palace of Love and Partnership / Palace of Self (with the Chancellor (Tian Fu) opposite) → a strong need for control.
•	Qi Sha + Zi Wei / Wu Qu / Lian Zhen (the Emperor / the Treasurer / the Star of Restraint) → more likely to lose control; especially Lian Zhen with Hua Ji. Because the Star of Restraint's "core quality is qiu (confinement)," it represents repression and restraint. The Star of Restraint in the Palace of Love and Partnership = at the same time wanting to restrain the partner; held in for long enough, when it meets Sha stars and Hua Ji, it may explode.
•	The Sun (Tai Yang) with Sha stars, or Zi Wei–Qi Sha + Tuo Luo → the Sun wants respect and face; if ignored or humiliated, it may go out of control.
•	The cold-violence type: ruminative stars + Ling Xing → for example, Tian Ji–Tian Liang meeting too many Sha stars and Hua Ji tends toward cold violence; it may not lay a hand on you, but it will keep you repressed for a long time.
•	Psychological oppression: the shelter stars (the Sun, the Moon (Tai Yin), the Guardian (Tian Liang)) with Hua Quan + Sha stars.

•	Each of the fourteen major stars has its own trigger point: for example, the Sun wants respect, the Treasurer values practical gain, the Star of Restraint wants rules. If a core need is denied for a long time, it may turn into violence.
→ If these combinations also get Sha stars and Hua Ji, the danger level is even higher.

③ Triggering in the timing charts: when will it erupt?
Potential in the chart ≠ violence will definitely happen, but in particular periods of the timing charts (= when the time comes), it may erupt.
1\.	Physical violence
• The Palace of Love and Partnership, Palace of Home and Property or Palace of Children and Creation overlapping the Palace of Body and Health → easily leads to "physical violence coming from the family or the relationship" (it's the same when overlapping palaces, flying transformations or hidden combinations cause problems in the Palace of Body and Health).

2\.	Psychological violence
• The Palace of Inner Life and Fortune is triggered, or the shelter stars (the Sun, the Moon, the Guardian) with Hua Quan + Sha stars → cold violence, humiliation, manipulation appear.
• The Blessing Star (Tian Tong) with Hua Quan or Hua Ke → these are all possible (control and psychological domination).

3\.	Stress-driven eruption
• Going through a Sha Po Lang ten-year cycle, the Moon with Hua Quan, the Guardian with Hua Quan → heavy pressure, even less stable emotions.

4\.	Bloodshed (bodily injury caused by violence)
In the timing charts, Hong Luan + Sha stars on the Self–Outer World / Parents–Body axis:
• + Qing Yang → open wounds, cuts involving a blade
• + Ling Xing → small wounds
• + Tuo Luo → bruises
• + Huo Xing → inflammation, redness and swelling
The more Sha stars, the more serious the injury.

So: personality tendencies in the chart × pressure triggered by the timing charts → violence & injury may happen.

④ Tai Sui ru gua: you can check even without their chart
If you can't get the other person's chart, you can use their zodiac animal to check with "Tai Sui ru gua":
1.	Map the other person's zodiac animal onto the Earthly Branch in your own chart (Rat = Zi, Ox = Chou…).
2.	Treat that palace as the other person's "Palace of Self."
• Two palaces counterclockwise = their Palace of Love and Partnership
• One palace clockwise = their Palace of Parents and Elders (Parents–Body axis)
3.	Check whether these palaces show the situations above.

Zi Wei Dou Shu can warn us in advance:
a dangerous partner doesn't "suddenly turn bad"; the seeds were planted in the chart long ago, and erupt when the timing comes.
Rather than falling in and then having to escape, see clearly before getting into the relationship!
