You've surely had moments like this too:
you want to lose weight, buy a pile of courses and equipment, and in the end only the bathroom scale gets unboxed, and you eat diet meals for a few days.
You have a startup idea in your head, chat about it happily with friends, but only think and never act, and in reality you're still at zero.

In the AI era, only three things truly set people apart:
1.	Seeing the real pain points
2.	Whether you dare to throw your ideas into the real world and execute them
3.	Whether you're willing to test and fail fast
All of these require the drive to act!

Many people ask themselves: "If my drive to act is this poor, am I just… hopeless?"
Not entirely.

Everyone has moments of wanting to "save energy," but life always has key moments that call for a 100-meter sprint.
If you keep talking without doing, let me teach you a method:
catch the right timing and give yourself a push.

→ Using the pairing of palaces and stars in Zi Wei Dou Shu, you can deliberately make yourself more driven to act "at the right times."

Through Winston's startup story, let's look at what drive to act looks like when it's "ignited."

01 | See a pain point → run a small experiment → throw it at the real world: the strongest way to get action started
Winston was a rookie lawyer at a big American law firm, handling small landlord-tenant cases, and knew nothing about the relevant California law.
One day, he saw his roommate playing games with GPT-3.
Most people would say: "Wow, that's fun."
But what he thought was: "Could this be used directly to solve legal problems?"

He didn't stop at "thinking"; he immediately ran a small experiment:
•	feeding California's residential tenancy laws into GPT-3
•	pulling 100 real legal questions from online forums and having GPT-3 answer them
•	then asking three practicing lawyers to grade the AI's answers
The result, according to the practicing lawyers: 86% of the answers could go straight to clients as they were.

In that moment, what he saw wasn't "fun AI";
he discovered the essence of starting a business: the advantages built up through accumulated experience suddenly weren't worth so much in the face of AI, & "the way the whole legal industry works could be rewritten."

Even bolder, he immediately sent the experiment data + a demo
to OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, copying OpenAI's general counsel, Jason Kwon (only a lawyer would understand what a breakthrough he was making).

Incredible execution → a rookie lawyer and a nobody in the startup world sent a cold email straight to Sam Altman!

Within 24 hours he got a reply, and his company, Harvey, received investment from the OpenAI Startup Fund.
Three years later, the company was valued at US$8 billion.

What's most striking about Winston's story isn't talent, but the rhythm of his actions:
see a pain point → run a small experiment right away → gather real-world feedback → test and fail fast → keep acting.

This is the energy of "Qing Yang + the Star of Depletion (Po Jun)": insight + decisiveness + breakthrough.

02 | The Star of Depletion, Qing Yang, Huo Xing: the three great detonators of action in your life
In Zi Wei Dou Shu, three stars correspond exactly to three kinds of action energy.

║ Qing Yang: action that cuts with a single stroke
Qing Yang has only three keywords: fast, ruthless, decisive.
It symbolizes raw decisiveness: no dragging, no hesitating, no going in circles.
Whichever palace this star enters, that scene gets the power of "cutting what should be cut, ending what should be ended." It's so in love, it's so in money management, and even more so at work.

You'll see that people with Qing Yang:
• do it as soon as they think of it, execute immediately, don't put it off until tomorrow
• do it when it's right, and don't hold on to it when it's wrong

This personality of "drawing a sword to cut water" is its greatest strength: fast action, crisp decisions.
But Qing Yang can also get hurt from charging straight in: it acts faster than it thinks, easily goes head-to-head, and tends to learn by trial and error firsthand.

The essence of Qing Yang is the positive side of the drive to act.
Strategies that suit you:
• set small short-term goals and move forward on speed
• before big decisions, find someone willing to tell you the truth to help you "hit the brakes"
• use action to create momentum, rather than letting impulse decide the outcome

Qing Yang gives you the courage for the first cut, but how it ends is up to you.

║ Huo Xing: an instant burst of energy, but hard to sustain
Huo Xing is like the fireworks of your life: explosive, quick to ignite, astonishingly bright, but short-lived.
In the chart, Huo Xing isn't just about emotions and temper; it symbolizes the concentration and release of instant energy.

Whichever palace Huo Xing sits in is where "bursts of charging ahead" easily happen:
• cramming at the last minute until four in the morning
• once inspiration strikes, being unable to stop
• passion that comes on fiercely, but fades just as fast
Once a Huo Xing person is triggered, they can jump straight to action.
That's also the problem: quick to ignite, quick to burn out.

Huo Xing's essence isn't "steady"; it's "explosive."
It's suited to:
• tasks that need speed
• instant action that needs to spark, break a deadlock, or push things forward
• short sprints, not long marathons

If you have Huo Xing in a palace:
• focus your firepower on the most important small area
• replace willpower with systems
• build an outside environment that helps you "keep up the rhythm" (tools, partners, reminders)
Huo Xing can push you very far in an instant, but you have to choose the right direction.

║ The Star of Depletion (Po Jun): the creativity and execution of great destruction and great building
The Star of Depletion is the closest of the 14 major stars to "the archetype of the entrepreneur": dreams × courage × the boldness to tear down the old world and start over.

The Star of Depletion's core quality is "hao" (depletion), not as in waste, but as in daring to pour all its energy into something new.

This star represents:
• charging in first
• being the most willing to take risks
• having no patience for old rules
• the more complex something is, the more it wants to take it apart and redo it
• what others find frightening, it finds thrilling

The Star of Depletion's action isn't the diligent kind, but the breakthrough kind.
Its speed comes from:
"I've understood this direction, I think it works, so I'm just doing it."

This is different from Qing Yang's impulsiveness, and different from Huo Xing's instant bursts.

The Star of Depletion's power is
rewriting based on insight, not an outburst based on emotion.

But the Star of Depletion's risk lies here too:
• wanting to flip the table too quickly
• the old has been thrown out, but the new isn't built yet
• strong passion, but strong depletion too, needing a support system to resupply it afterward

What the Star of Depletion needs to learn isn't to become steady, but to:
• build a backup
• leave a next path open after a great tearing-down

Beneath the Star of Depletion's abundant passion is "the courage to change."

03 | It's not that you lack drive; you're just not acting at your "timing for action"
If right now
you clearly want to lose weight but can't,
you clearly want to start a business but you're stuck,
you clearly know what you should do but keep procrastinating,

make good use of the timing charts to find "the times when it's easiest for you to get moving":
as your timing charts turn with time and space, when
•	the Palace of Self in the timing charts
•	the Palace of the Outer World in the timing charts
•	the Palace of Daily Focus in the timing charts
•	the Palace of Body and Health in the timing charts
contain
the Star of Depletion, Qing Yang or Huo Xing,
your drive to act will become noticeably stronger, faster and more decisive.

This is the best time to launch plans:
•	want to turn the ideas in your head into reality → use the Star of Depletion in the Palace of Self / Palace of Daily Focus
•	want to cut off a bad relationship → use Qing Yang in the Palace of Love and Partnership
•	don't feel like studying for finals → when Huo Xing comes, do a short sprint
•	want to slim down, want to declutter → when Qing Yang comes in the timing charts, you'll definitely make that cut

04 | ​Conclusion
Everyone has the drive to act, and everyone has lazy times too.
Everyone's chart also has the Star of Depletion, Qing Yang and Huo Xing.
The difference isn't that you "don't have" the drive to act,
but whether, at "the right point in time,"
in the moment when time, space, environment and your own state are all "right," you can give yourself a push.

When the Zi Wei Dou Shu timing charts turn to the right palaces and the right stars,
that's often when it's easiest for you to break through, decide and burst into action.

The Star of Depletion gives you the courage to change,
Qing Yang gives you the courage to decide,
Huo Xing gives you the courage to charge.

It's not that you have no drive to act;
it's just that you used to push yourself forward at the wrong times.
