Why is the Star of Restraint (Lian Zhen) so hard to understand?
Because unlike the other major stars, it doesn't have a clear "thing it pursues."
The Emperor (Zi Wei) is very clear:
→ I want status, I want dignity (very clear coordinates)
The Treasurer (Wu Qu) is very clear too:
→ I care about money, about returns, about whether it's worth it (a very clear goal)

So what about the Star of Restraint?
What exactly is the Star of Restraint?
The answer is actually quite counterintuitive:
the essence of the Star of Restraint isn't desire, but "boundaries."

║ Why did the old texts write so badly of the Star of Restraint?
Legal trouble, sexual scandal, disputes, qiu (confinement).
It's not that the ancients wrote carelessly, but because
the Star of Restraint most easily shows up in one kind of place: arenas where human-made systems aren't yet fully established.
Such as:
• the gray areas of officialdom
• the boundaries where powers meet
• places where the law doesn't reach feelings, and feelings don't enter the law
In these blurry zones,

the Star of Restraint seems to be toying with desire,
but in fact it's doing something very difficult: "boundary risk management."

║ So what exactly is "qiu"?
"A person," shut inside a frame.
Many people see that the Star of Restraint's core quality is "qiu"
and assume it means being locked up, being sued, bad fortune.

But the Star of Restraint's "qiu"
isn't some external cage;
it's a measuring stick of restraint built into the heart.

The Star of Restraint has long since set its own standards.
In its heart, it keeps weighing:
→ If I do this, have I crossed the line?
→ This relationship, does it count or not?
→ I know there's a shortcut, but can I take it?

That's why the Star of Restraint is so often misunderstood.

People with the Star of Restraint aren't without morals;
they know all too well where the loopholes in morality are.

People with the Star of Restraint don't want to break the rules;
they know all too well where the rules are incomplete.

You'll see many "clean" Star of Restraint patterns (with Hua Lu, or meeting Lu Cun) (or a normal Star of Restraint that doesn't meet Sha stars or Hua Ji)
that look compliant, manage themselves well, and have both principles and achievements:
every step they take has to live up to the measuring stick in their heart.

There's one more key point you must remember when learning the Star of Restraint:
it's very good at recognizing risks and confirming boundaries.

In the end, whether it takes the risk, whether it steps over the line, whether it plays with the rules,
you must look at the stars in the opposite palace or the same palace.

When there's a chance, let's talk more about the Star of Restraint.
