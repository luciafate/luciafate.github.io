║ Why can Zi Wei Dou Shu read signs of the future?
In real life, everything that happens comes down to three basic elements:
people, time and space.

People: determine action and direction, who did what.
Time: determines when things happen.
Space: determines where things happen, and in what environment.

Simply put:
person (personality and abilities) + time (the timing) + space (the environment and setting) = outcome (how events develop)

Zi Wei Dou Shu uses this same formula to look at life.

The stars represent "people": they reflect personality, values and behavioral habits.
The palaces represent "space": they symbolize the area of life where events happen, for example, the Palace of Love and Partnership is relationships, and the Palace of Daily Focus is career.
The Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches represent "time": they symbolize the push and triggering of time, determining when change will happen.

The reason Zi Wei Dou Shu can predict "what may happen in the future"
is that it can analyze
what tendencies and actions this person is most likely to show at this kind of time and in this kind of environment.

What the chart shows isn't fate itself, but:

the result of your habits × present conditions × the push of time.

This is what we call "signs."

Does that make sense?
The first step of Zi Wei Dou Shu is to help you know yourself.
The next step is to help you see
how events may unfold in the future once your personality meets changes in time and environment.

If you can see it coming, you have a chance to change it.
That is the real power of Zi Wei Dou Shu.
