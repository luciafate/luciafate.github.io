Many people say the Resolute Star (Qi Sha) is heartless.
In fact, that's not it at all.
Very often, you're actually the one who takes commitments most seriously.

The problem is:
you take the "loyalty" of making your way in the world and use it to run an "intimate relationship" that needs softness.

When Resolute Star people are in love, it's actually a lot like fighting a war.
Once you've decided someone is one of your own, you bring them within the scope of your responsibility and care.

When something goes wrong, you charge in first.
When there's a mess, you clean it up.
The responsibility, you carry.

But precisely because of this, the Resolute Star in a relationship often walks into a fixed three-part sequence.

Stage one | Holding the line to the end
When the relationship runs into trouble,
the Resolute Star rarely talks about feelings first.
Its first reaction is usually: give twice as much.
Work harder to keep the relationship going, fight harder to solve the problem.

Inside, there's really only one thought:
"If I just hold on a little longer, maybe the relationship won't fall apart."

Stage two | Lonely calculation
This is the most agonizing stage.
You begin to realize
your "firmness" can't buy the other person's "growth."
Your "commitment" has become their "dependence."

So you gradually fall silent.
Inside, you're weighing things up, calmly and painfully:
"This isn't loyalty anymore, and it isn't a healthy interaction."
"This is a bottomless whirlpool."

Stage three | Cutting your losses in tears
Many people think the Resolute Star turns cold suddenly.
It doesn't.
The real Resolute Star has usually already held on for a very long time.
Once you're sure this relationship is nothing but depletion, and the other person has no chance of changing,
at that moment,
the Resolute Star instantly takes back every umbrella of protection.
That seemingly cold "cutting it off just like that" is, after holding on for so long,
a final act of mercy.

Everyone's chart actually has the Resolute Star.
Which palace does your Resolute Star fall in?
