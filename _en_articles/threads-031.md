The "Palace of Inner Life and Fortune" can show a person's worldview, outlook on life and values, inner world, fortune and later years,
but did you know?
It also perfectly gives away your "taste in spending"! (It's opposite the Palace of Money Management.)

I'm a living example myself.
In my natal chart, the Palace of Inner Life and Fortune is "Zi Wei–Tian Fu" (the Emperor with the Chancellor), and the Money–Inner Life axis also has a "Wen Chang" stuck on it.
Put together, I'm a pretentious artsy hipster through and through (admitting it up front🤣).

Like a lot of people, I lose my head super easily, and after buying a pile of useless little things I want to chop my hands off. Especially with "fun, quality little trinkets" (above all Japanese ones), I!! have zero resistance.

And sure enough, I recently slipped up and bought something again… um, it's an omamori (a protective charm).

✨[Ten Thousand Galloping Horses series] New Year crabapple-blossom good-luck omamori✨
The best part! It's made from "crabapple-blossom glass" salvaged from old Taiwanese buildings! Every piece has a different pattern and color, carrying the warmth of a one-of-a-kind moment in time aaah (my artsy soul is ablaze🔥).

And how cute is this omamori~ "Success arrives with the horse"! Just looking at it is healing…… even if it hurts my wallet.
