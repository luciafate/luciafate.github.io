Lucia · One-on-one written chart reading

When life presents a hard question, use your chart to see the problem, and the possibilities of different choices, more clearly

An Imperial Astrology (Zi Wei Dou Shu) chart is drawn from your time of birth. It shows the resources, habits, and life lessons you carry with you.

When something in life just won't make sense, or you are stuck between choices and can't decide, I'll look at your chart together with your real circumstances to help you see the core of the problem, how things might develop, and what to watch out for, and I'll offer my suggestions for your consideration.

I don't decide the answer for you. I help you see where different choices may lead, so you can make your own decision on firmer ground.

Lucia does not collect this NT$500 donation. You choose the charity and make the donation yourself.

From your questions to your written report

  1. Send your details and questions

    Please provide:

    • Your date of birth (Gregorian calendar)
    • Your place of birth: city and country, plus state or province if needed
    • Your time of birth as local clock time at your birthplace, to the hour and minute if possible
    • Whether that time is exact, approximate, or unknown
    • Your sex at birth
    • The name you'd like to be called
    • The three questions you'd like to ask

    Please give the local clock time recorded at your birthplace. Do not convert it to Taiwan time.

  2. Confirm your details and questions

    Lucia will first confirm your chart details and questions. If a question needs more background or a clearer focus, she will check with you first.

  3. Pay the deposit

    Once your details and three questions are confirmed, pay the NT$500 deposit. After receiving it, Lucia will set up your chart and begin writing your report.

  4. Receive your report

    The report takes about 3–5 working days. When it's finished, Lucia will send you the full written report in English as a PDF.

  5. Read the report and pay the balance

    After you've read the PDF report, pay the remaining NT$500 of the reading fee.

  6. Make your charity donation

    Donate the other NT$500 in your own name to a charity of your choice. You don't need to provide proof; just let Lucia know once it's done.

  7. Ask a follow-up question

    If anything in the analysis is still unclear, you may ask one follow-up question about this reading within one week of receiving the report.

The more specific your questions, the more the analysis can focus on what you really need to deal with

Think your three questions through first. Where possible, describe your current situation, what has already happened, and the options you're considering.

✕ Is my career luck good?

✓ I've been in my current role for three years and recently received another job offer. The new job pays more but is less stable. I'd like to understand what developments and risks I might face if I stay, and if I switch.

The more focused your questions, the more specific and useful Lucia's analysis of your real situation can be.
